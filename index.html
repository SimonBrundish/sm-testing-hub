How the numbers are calculated. Percentiles rank each athlete against the squad shown (or their own gender in profiles and category scores) at their most recent test. Category scores average the percentiles of the tests that make up Speed (10m Sprint, 30m Fly), Strength (TB Deadlift Power), Bounce (CMJ, Single-Leg CMJ, Broad Jump), Agility (Raducanu Lateral Agility) and Crawl (Pentagon Crawl Ratio). Pace pills compare each athlete's tracked development rate for that category against their squad.
